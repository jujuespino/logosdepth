# Notices and attributions

## Scripture text

Verse text in `data/verses.json` is generated by `scripts/build_content.py` from public datasets:

- **King James Version (1769)**: text from the `es-kjv` package. Public domain in the United States. Some jurisdictions (notably the United Kingdom) treat the KJV differently; confirm before distributing there.
- **World English Bible (WEB)**: public domain, released by Rainbow Missions, Inc. (<https://worldenglish.bible/>), obtained via the `midvash/bible-data` repository.
- **Strong's number tagging of the KJV** (used to find the exact original-language words in each verse): CrossWire Bible Society KJV module, via `@metaxia/scriptures-source-crosswire-kjv`.

The first 27 verses entered by hand were checked against the 1769 text: 26 matched exactly and 1 (John 14:6, an omitted opening clause) was corrected.

## Original-language word data

- **Curated entries (`data/lexicon.json`, 83 words):** Strong's numbers and original-language lemmas were audited against STEPBible: 82 of 83 were correct; `G265` was mislabeled and is now `G266` (hamartia). Occurrence counts were cross-checked against the KJV Strong's tagging and three unsupported counts were corrected. Short glosses and KJV renderings are consistent with the data; the longer "in depth" explanations are original prose written for this project.

  > **Open item:** the in-depth explanations should be reviewed for theological and linguistic accuracy by a qualified person before public release.

- **Brief entries (`data/lexicon_brief.json`):** lemma, transliteration and gloss come from **STEPBible** (Tyndale House, Cambridge, UK; <https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data>), TBESG (Greek, based on Abbott-Smith) and TBESH (Hebrew, based on Brown-Driver-Briggs), licensed **CC BY 4.0**. Attribution is required wherever this data is shown; the app shows it in each brief entry and a credits page is planned.
