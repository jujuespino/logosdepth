# Notices and attributions

## Scripture text

Verse text in `data/verses.json` is from the **King James Version (KJV)**, which is in the public domain in the United States. Some jurisdictions (notably the United Kingdom) treat the KJV differently; confirm before distributing there.

> **Open item:** the current verse texts were entered by hand and need a proofread against a trusted source before public release.

## Original-language word data

`data/lexicon.json` contains Hebrew and Greek word entries keyed by Strong's numbers.

> **Open item:** the provenance and license of the definitions, extended definitions, and usage counts have not yet been documented. Strong's numbering and classical lexicons such as Thayer's and Brown-Driver-Briggs are public domain, but each field in this file must be traced to its source (or confirmed as original text) before public release.
